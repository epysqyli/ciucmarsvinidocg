The winery

A story that goes back a long way

We are a small family-run winery on the hills of Diano d'Alba, where the Tanaro fog meets marl and sand. Three hectares of vineyard, tended by hand, and a single ambition: wines that tell of our corner of the Langhe.

For three generations our family has farmed these vines; since 2022 we have been steering them ourselves — new eyes, the same respect for the land.
