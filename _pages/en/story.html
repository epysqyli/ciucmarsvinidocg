The Bricco Lupo story begins long before the winery: it begins with our grandparents, who walked these hills on foot, among rows planted back when “Barolo” was just the name of a village.

For years, the grapes from our vines went to the great cellars of the area. In 2022 we decided to try going it alone: three inherited hectares, a harvest full of courage, and a new roof over dad's old barrels.

“Small by choice: we would rather know every row by name.”

Today we work as a family: in the vineyard almost everything is done by hand; in the cellar we disturb the wine as little as possible. Indigenous yeasts, sulphur reduced to a minimum, large oak only when it helps the wine breathe — never to mask it.

We are in no hurry. Nebbiolo chases time, and we run behind it.
