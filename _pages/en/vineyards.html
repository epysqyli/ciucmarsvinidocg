Our vines sit on two different hills: one parcel in Diano d'Alba, on the sun-washed, south-facing sorì that give Dolcetto fruit and immediacy; one in La Morra, on calcareous clay marls that give Nebbiolo perfume and grace.

South and south-west exposures, between 380 and 480 metres: up here the Tanaro fog rolls in punctually every evening, and the day-night temperature swing keeps the aromas quiet-spoken.

We use no herbicides; the rows are grassed with spontaneous cover crops, copper and sulphur only when needed. The rest is up to the seasons.
